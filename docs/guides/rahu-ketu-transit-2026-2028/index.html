Rahu Ketu Transit 2026 to 2028: Rahu in Capricorn, Ketu in Cancer

By the Natal Notes team. Published November 22, 2026. Sidereal zodiac (Lahiri), times in US Eastern.

Rahu moves into sidereal Capricorn and Ketu into Cancer on December 5, 2026, and they stay until June 23, 2028. For about eighteen months, the hunger in your chart points at work, status and long-term structure, while the letting go happens around home, family and emotional safety. Where it lands depends on your rising sign.

Rahu and Ketu are the two points where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's. They aren't planets, but Vedic astrology treats them as the most karmic forces in a chart. They always sit exactly opposite each other and always move backward through the zodiac, changing signs about every eighteen months. So when Rahu changes sign, Ketu changes sign too, and a whole axis of your life switches on at once.

What do Rahu and Ketu actually do?

Rahu is appetite. Wherever it travels it magnifies wanting: more recognition, more money, more experience, often through unusual or foreign routes. It can bring fast progress and it can bring obsession. Ketu is the opposite pull. It loosens your grip, takes the flavour out of what used to matter, and turns you inward. Ketu is often where people discover they've already outgrown something.

The useful way to read a node transit is as a pair. Rahu shows the area of life you'll be pushed to build, and Ketu shows the area you'll be asked to simplify. Fighting either one tends to be what makes the period feel hard.

What does Rahu in Capricorn mean?

Capricorn is Saturn's sign: effort, rules, institutions, reputation, and slow results that last. Rahu here wants status and it wants it to count. Expect the collective mood to fixate on work, power structures, government, ambition and who gets to climb. On a personal level, it rewards steady, disciplined effort more than shortcuts, which is not Rahu's usual style. Rahu in an earth sign of Saturn has to slow down to get what it wants.

Rahu starts at the very end of the sign, 29° Capricorn, and backs up through three nakshatras:

  • Dhanishta (ruled by Mars) from December 2026 to mid-April 2027: drive, rhythm, wealth, a competitive edge.
  • Shravana (ruled by the Moon) from mid-April to mid-December 2027: listening, learning, reputation through what people hear about you.
  • Uttara Ashadha (ruled by the Sun) from mid-December 2027 to June 2028: the final, hard-won kind of victory.

What does Ketu in Cancer mean?

Cancer is the Moon's sign: home, mother, belonging, the feeling of being emotionally safe. Ketu here can make familiar comforts feel strangely empty. It's a common time to move house, change how you relate to family, or stop needing a place or a person to feel okay. It's also a strong placement for inner work, because Ketu in a water sign turns feeling into insight.

Ketu moves through Ashlesha (ruled by Mercury) until mid-August 2027, Pushya (ruled by Saturn) until late April 2028, then the end of Punarvasu (ruled by Jupiter).

If this period stirs up grief about family or home, or old feelings that are heavy to carry, please talk to someone: a counsellor, a doctor, or a person you trust. Astrology can name a season. It can't carry it for you.

Traditionally, the nodes do their best work in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th houses. Rahu lands in one of those for Aries, Leo, Scorpio and Pisces rising, which makes this a strong stretch for ambition, and Ketu does for Taurus, Virgo, Libra and Aquarius rising, which makes letting go easier. It's a more inward, demanding period for Cancer and Capricorn rising, who have the nodes on the 1st and 7th houses, the axis of self and partner.

Which dates matter most?

  • December 5, 2026: Rahu enters Capricorn and Ketu enters Cancer.
  • January 24, 2027: Jupiter, already retrograde, moves back into Cancer and joins Ketu. Jupiter meets Ketu exactly at 25°27' Cancer on March 1, 2027, and again at 22°49' Cancer on April 19, 2027. Jupiter with Ketu is a classic spiritual pairing: wisdom without much interest in reward.
  • February 6, 2027: an annular solar eclipse at 23°23' Capricorn, close to Rahu. The first big eclipse of the cycle, and a strong new start for the Rahu house in your chart.
  • June 2, 2027: Saturn enters Aries, from where it aspects Capricorn. Saturn, the ruler of Capricorn, now watches over Rahu, which tends to make shortcuts cost more. Saturn backs into Pisces on October 19, 2027 and returns to Aries on February 23, 2028.
  • August 2, 2027: a total solar eclipse at 15°40' Cancer, near Ketu. This is the most dramatic eclipse of the cycle, a reset for the Ketu house in your chart.
  • January 7, 2028: Mars, exalted in Capricorn, meets Rahu at 8°54' Capricorn. A forceful, impatient day; good for decisive action, poor for arguments.
  • January 26, 2028: an annular solar eclipse at 11°55' Capricorn, the last major Capricorn eclipse of the cycle.
  • June 23, 2028: Rahu moves into Sagittarius and Ketu into Gemini.

How do I work with this transit instead of against it?

Treat Rahu's house as a long project, not a sprint. Capricorn rewards structure: a plan, a schedule, a skill you build month by month. And treat Ketu's house as a place to edit. What can you stop doing, owning or proving there?

Questions

When does Rahu enter Capricorn in 2026?

Using the mean node and the Lahiri zodiac, Rahu moves from Aquarius into Capricorn on December 5, 2026, around midday US Eastern time. Ketu moves from Leo into Cancer at the same moment. Calendars that use the true node can show a slightly different day.

How long will Rahu stay in Capricorn?

About eighteen and a half months. Rahu leaves Capricorn for Sagittarius, and Ketu leaves Cancer for Gemini, on June 23, 2028.

Is Rahu in Capricorn good or bad?

Neither, on its own. Rahu amplifies the house it passes through in your chart. Traditional Vedic astrology says the nodes give their best results in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th houses from your rising sign, and ask more of you elsewhere.

Which eclipses happen during this Rahu Ketu transit?

The main ones fall on the Capricorn and Cancer axis: an annular solar eclipse on February 6, 2027 in Capricorn, a total solar eclipse on August 2, 2027 in Cancer, and an annular solar eclipse on January 26, 2028 in Capricorn.

Should I read this for my sun sign or my rising sign?

Rising sign first. Vedic transits are read by the house they fall in from your ascendant. Your moon sign is the second reading, and your sun sign the third.
