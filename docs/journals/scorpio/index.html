The Scorpio pattern

Scorpio feels everything and shows almost none of it. Many Scorpios test people instead of telling them, and hold on to a hurt long after the person who caused it has moved on.

What are the Scorpio dates?

In the tropical zodiac most horoscopes use, Scorpio runs roughly Oct 23 – Nov 21. In sidereal astrology, which follows where the constellations sit today, it runs roughly Nov 16 – Dec 15. Exact dates shift by a day or so from year to year.

What is Scorpio's element and ruling planet?

Scorpio is a fixed water sign, ruled by Mars (modern ruler Pluto).
