What's the big picture for a Virgo Moon right now?

Saturn is in Pisces, your 7th house from the Moon: marriage, business partners and anyone you deal with one-on-one. It has been retrograde, pulling old relationship questions back for review, and it aspects your 1st house (your body and mood), 4th (home) and 9th (beliefs). Good news first: this is not Sade Sati, which only begins when Saturn reaches the sign before your Moon.

Jupiter sits in Leo, your 12th house from the Moon, together with Mars. The 12th is the house of rest, sleep, spending and the inner life. Classical transit rules count Jupiter in the 12th from the Moon as one of its quieter positions: generous, spiritual, good for retreat and travel abroad, but easy on the wallet in the wrong way. From Leo, Jupiter aspects your 4th, 6th and 8th houses.

The nodes change signs in this window, the biggest shift of the year for you. Rahu leaves your 6th house and Ketu leaves your 12th.

What does Venus in Libra mean for a Virgo Moon on November 22?

Venus enters Libra, its own sign, on November 22 and stays there through the whole window. For a Virgo Moon that's your 2nd house: money, family, food and the way you speak. Venus at home here is one of the easiest placements you'll get all year. It favours earning, pricing your work properly, family gatherings, and saying hard things kindly. Venus also rules your 9th house, so a mentor, teacher or trip can help your finances.

What does the Full Moon in Taurus mean for a Virgo Moon on November 24?

The Full Moon on November 24 falls at 8°06' Taurus, in Krittika nakshatra, in your 9th house from the Moon. The 9th is beliefs, teachers, higher study, long journeys and luck. Krittika is ruled by the Sun and is known as the blade that cuts away what's false. It's a good night to notice what you no longer believe about yourself, your faith or your future, and to write it down. Uranus is in Taurus too, so the insight may arrive suddenly.

What does Mercury in Scorpio mean for a Virgo Moon on December 2?

Mercury is your ruling planet, so every move it makes is personal. Mercury enters Scorpio, your 3rd house from the Moon, on December 2. The 3rd house is your voice: messages, writing, short trips, siblings and the nerve to say the thing. The Sun is already here, and classical rules count the Sun in the 3rd from the Moon as one of its best transits: confidence, energy and wins through effort. Use this stretch for research, editing, pitching and honest emails.

What does Rahu in Capricorn mean for a Virgo Moon on December 5?

On December 5, Rahu enters Capricorn and Ketu enters Cancer, where they'll stay until mid-2028. For a Virgo Moon, Rahu now lands in your 5th house: romance, creativity, children, study and anything you do for joy. Ketu lands in your 11th house: friends, networks, income from your work and long-term goals.

You'll want more pleasure, more creative risk and more romance, sometimes urgently. Some friendships or group commitments may lose their pull; that's Ketu editing your social life, not ending it. Rahu leaving your 6th house can also loosen a work rivalry or a draining routine. (Our full guide to the Rahu Ketu transit covers every sign and every eclipse of the cycle.)

What does the New Moon in Scorpio mean for a Virgo Moon on December 8?

The New Moon on December 8 falls at 22°42' Scorpio, in Jyeshtha nakshatra, in your 3rd house. Jyeshtha is ruled by Mercury, your own planet, which makes this the strongest reset of the window for anything you write, teach, pitch or post. Start the newsletter, send the proposal, sign up for the class.

It's also the best night to start a journal. Write one sentence you've been rehearsing for months. If you write it in the Virgo Daily Journal, the affirmation at the top of the page will be waiting for you.

What does Saturn turning direct mean for a Virgo Moon on December 10?

Saturn stations direct at 13°41' Pisces on December 10, in Uttara Bhadrapada nakshatra, in your 7th house from the Moon. After months of reviewing relationships and agreements, things start moving again. If you've been waiting on a partner to commit, a contract to be signed, or yourself to decide what a relationship is, Saturn's turn on December 10 makes answers easier to reach. Saturn rewards the agreements you're willing to put in writing and keep.

What does Jupiter retrograde in Leo mean for a Virgo Moon on December 12?

Jupiter stations retrograde at 2°47' Leo on December 12, in Magha nakshatra, in your 12th house from the Moon, and stays retrograde into 2027. This is a turn inward: quiet study, therapy or spiritual practice, real rest, and an honest look at where money leaks. It isn't the moment for an expensive move abroad on impulse. The same evening, Neptune turns direct at 7°22' Pisces in your 7th house, which can make a foggy relationship feel clearer.

With Mars also in your 12th, sleep and energy can run low. Protect your rest, and if tiredness or poor sleep hangs on, check in with a doctor rather than pushing through.

What does the Sun in Sagittarius mean for a Virgo Moon on December 15?

The Sun enters Sagittarius on December 15, moving into your 4th house from the Moon: home, family, mother and inner peace. Your attention turns to where you live and who you live with: holiday hosting, an overdue family talk, or making your space feel like yours again. Mercury follows the Sun into Sagittarius on December 21. Mercury in the 4th from the Moon is a classically good transit, so the last days of this window favour working from home, planning home projects and talking to a parent.

Just after this window, the Full Moon on December 23 at 7°59' Gemini lights up your 10th house of career, a clear read on what your work has added up to this year.

Love and relationships

Your love life runs on two tracks. Saturn turning direct in your 7th house on December 10 favours serious partnership: defining things, moving in, plans that last. Rahu entering your 5th on December 5 favours chemistry, flirting and creative dates, and can make you crave excitement. Single? An attraction that feels unusual for you is worth exploring slowly. Partnered? Bring play back in rather than looking for it elsewhere. Venus in your 2nd softens how you speak to the people closest to you, and Mars aspecting your 7th from the 12th means tension may show up as tiredness or silence. Name it early.

Career and money

Mercury, which rules your 10th house of career, spends this window in your 3rd and 4th houses: a month for groundwork, outreach, learning and fixing the home side of work-life balance. With Venus at home in your 2nd house, review what you charge and what you spend. Jupiter and Mars in your 12th can make the holidays expensive, so set a budget before the Sun enters Sagittarius on December 15.

Best days and slow days

Best: November 24 (the Full Moon in your 9th: clarity on beliefs and direction), December 8 (the New Moon in Mercury's nakshatra: start something you'll write or say), and December 10 (Saturn turns direct: relationship decisions).

Slow: December 5 (Rahu and Ketu change signs, which can feel unsettled) and December 12 (Jupiter turns retrograde: rest, don't overspend).

Questions

Why is this horoscope written for the Virgo Moon sign?

In Vedic astrology your Moon sign, or rashi, is the sign used for transit forecasts, and it's what most people in India mean by 'my sign'. Transits are counted from the natal Moon because the Moon is the mind, where you feel a transit first. If you have Virgo rising, read this a second time from your ascendant.

Is this a good time for Virgo to change jobs?

It's better for preparing than leaping. Mercury, your ruling planet, spends this stretch in your 3rd and 4th houses from the Moon, which favours updating your CV, writing and conversations. The Full Moon on December 23 in your 10th house, the day after this window, shows what's working at work before you decide.

Is Virgo Moon in Sade Sati in 2026?

No. Sade Sati is Saturn's transit through the 12th, 1st and 2nd houses from your Moon, which for a Virgo Moon means Leo, Virgo and Libra. Saturn is in Pisces, your 7th house from the Moon, so it shapes partnerships rather than your whole life.

How do I find my sidereal Moon sign?

You need your birth date, birth time and the time zone you were born in, because the Moon changes sign every two to two and a half days. Our free sign finder works it out in your browser and tells you if the Moon changed sign on your birthday.
