What's the big picture for Virgo rising in December?

Three slow planets set the tone. Saturn sits in Pisces, your 7th house of marriage, business partners and anyone you deal with one-on-one. It has been retrograde, which tends to bring old relationship questions back for review. Saturn aspects your 1st house (your body and confidence), your 4th (home) and your 9th (beliefs and long-range plans), so the pressure in partnerships spills into how you feel day to day.

Jupiter is in Leo, your 12th house of rest, spending and the inner life, along with Mars. The 12th is a quiet house. Jupiter here favours retreat, spiritual practice, travel abroad and generosity, but it can make money slip away on things you won't remember buying. Jupiter rules your 4th and 7th houses, so its mood shows up at home and with your partner too. From Leo, Jupiter aspects your 4th, 6th (work routines and health habits) and 8th (shared money and change).

The nodes change signs this month, the biggest shift of the year for you. Rahu leaves your 6th house and Ketu leaves your 12th.

What does Mercury in Scorpio mean for Virgo on December 2?

Mercury is your ruling planet, so every move it makes is personal. Mercury enters Scorpio, your 3rd house, on December 2. The 3rd house is your voice: messages, writing, short trips, siblings, neighbours, and the nerve to say the thing. Mercury in Scorpio makes your words sharper and more probing. Use it for research, editing and honest emails. Watch for saying more than you mean in a group chat.

What does Rahu in Capricorn mean for Virgo on December 5?

On December 5, Rahu enters Capricorn and Ketu enters Cancer, where they'll stay until June 2028. For Virgo rising, Rahu now lands in your 5th house: romance, creativity, children, study, hobbies and anything you do for joy. Ketu lands in your 11th house: friends, networks, income from your work and long-term goals.

In plain terms, you'll want more pleasure, more creative risk and more romance, sometimes urgently. You may also notice some friendships or group commitments losing their pull. That's Ketu editing your social life, not ending it. Rahu leaving your 6th house can mean a work rivalry or a draining routine that dominated the last eighteen months finally loosens its grip. What does the New Moon in Scorpio mean for Virgo on December 8?

The New Moon on December 8 falls at 22°42' Scorpio, in Jyeshtha nakshatra, in your 3rd house. Jyeshtha is ruled by Mercury, your own planet, which makes this a strong reset for anything you write, teach, pitch or post. Start the newsletter, send the proposal, sign up for the class.

This is also the best night of the month to start a journal. Pick one sentence you've been rehearsing for months and write it down. If you're doing it in the Virgo Daily Journal, the affirmation on the page will be waiting for you.

What does Saturn turning direct mean for Virgo on December 10?

Saturn stations direct at 13°41' Pisces on December 10, in Uttara Bhadrapada nakshatra, in your 7th house. After months of reviewing relationships and agreements, things start moving forward again. If you've been waiting on a partner to commit, on a contract to be signed, or on yourself to decide what a relationship is, Saturn's turn on December 10 is when answers come more easily. Saturn rewards the agreements you're willing to put in writing and keep.

What does Jupiter retrograde in Leo mean for Virgo on December 12?

Jupiter stations retrograde at 2°47' Leo on December 12, in Magha nakshatra, in your 12th house. It stays retrograde into 2027. Jupiter retrograde in your 12th is a turn inward: a good time for quiet study, therapy or spiritual practice, rest, and reviewing where your money is leaking. It isn't the moment for a big, expensive move abroad on impulse.

The same evening, Neptune turns direct at 7°22' Pisces in your 7th house, which can make a relationship feel clearer after a foggy stretch.

With Mars also in your 12th, sleep and energy can run low this month. Protect your rest. If tiredness or poor sleep hangs on, it's worth checking in with a doctor rather than pushing through.

What does the Sun in Sagittarius mean for Virgo on December 15?

The Sun enters Sagittarius on December 15, moving into your 4th house of home, family and inner peace. Your attention turns to where you live and who you live with: holiday hosting, a family conversation that's overdue, or simply making your space feel like yours again. Mercury follows the Sun into Sagittarius on December 21, so the second half of the month is a good time to work from home, plan home projects, and talk to a parent.

What does the Full Moon in Gemini mean for Virgo on December 23?

The Full Moon on December 23 falls at 7°59' Gemini, in Ardra nakshatra, in your 10th house of career and reputation, opposite the Sun in your 4th. Full Moons show results. This one shows what your work has added up to this year, and whether your career and your home life are pulling in the same direction.

Ardra is ruled by Rahu and is known as a storm that clears the air. If something at work comes to a head, let it. You'll see more clearly afterward. Write down what happened, because you'll want those notes when you plan January.

Love and relationships

Your love life runs on two tracks this month. Saturn turning direct in your 7th house on December 10 favours serious, committed partnership: defining things, moving in, making plans that last. Rahu entering your 5th on December 5 favours chemistry, flirting and creative dates, and it can make you crave excitement. If you're single, an attraction that feels unusual for you is worth exploring slowly. If you're partnered, bring play back in instead of looking for it elsewhere.

Venus spends the whole month in Libra, its own sign, in your 2nd house of family, money and speech. That softens how you speak to the people closest to you. Mars aspects your 7th house from your 12th, so tension with a partner may come out as tiredness or silence. Name it early.

Career and money

Mercury, which rules your 10th house of career, spends December in your 3rd and 4th houses, so this is a month for groundwork: writing, outreach, learning a skill, and thinking about the home side of work-life balance. The Full Moon on December 23 in your 10th house is the month's career turning point.

With Venus in your 2nd house all month, it's a good time to review what you charge and what you spend. Jupiter and Mars in your 12th house can make December expensive, especially around the holidays, so set a budget before December 15 when the Sun moves into your home sector.

Best days and slow days

Best: December 8 (the New Moon in Mercury's nakshatra: start something you'll write or say), December 10 (Saturn turns direct: relationship decisions), and December 23 (the Full Moon in your 10th: career clarity).

Slow: December 5 (Rahu and Ketu change signs, which can feel unsettled) and December 12 (Jupiter turns retrograde: rest, don't overspend).

Write this month

At the Full Moon on December 23, write three things your work proved this year and one thing you're finished proving. Virgo's line in the journal fits the month: I am not less for making mistakes. I am more because I learn from them.

Questions

Is December 2026 a good month for Virgo to change jobs?

It's better for preparing than leaping. Mercury, which rules your career house, spends most of the month in your 3rd and 4th houses, so it favours updating your CV, writing, and conversations. The Full Moon on December 23 in your 10th house shows what's working at work and what isn't, which makes a decision in early January better informed.

What does Rahu in the 5th house mean for Virgo rising?

From December 5, 2026 until mid-2028, Rahu in Capricorn amplifies your 5th house: creativity, romance, children, study and taking risks for joy. Expect a stronger appetite for things that feel like play, and the occasional obsession. Steady creative routines help it more than impulsive bets.

What are good journal prompts for Virgo in December 2026?

At the New Moon on December 8, write what you want to say out loud this winter and haven't. At the Full Moon on December 23, write three things your work is proving and one thing you're done proving. The Virgo Daily Journal gives you a page and an affirmation for each.

Should I read this for my Virgo sun or my Virgo rising?

Rising first, because the houses in this forecast are counted from your ascendant. Then read it for your Virgo moon. If Virgo is only your sidereal sun sign (the Sun is in sidereal Virgo from mid-September to mid-October), it still describes your sense of purpose this month, just less precisely.
