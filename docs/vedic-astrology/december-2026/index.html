Vedic Astrology December 2026: Sidereal Transits, Dates and Eclipses

By the Natal Notes team. Published November 26, 2026. Sidereal zodiac (Lahiri), times in US Eastern.

The quick read

  • The big theme: the nodes change signs on December 5, as Rahu enters Capricorn and Ketu enters Cancer for about eighteen months.
  • The turn: Saturn stations direct on December 10 and Jupiter stations retrograde on December 12, two days apart.
  • Love: Venus spends the whole month in its own sign, Libra, the steadiest note in the sky.
  • Lunations: New Moon in Scorpio on December 8 (Jyeshtha), Full Moon in Gemini on December 23 (Ardra). No eclipse this month.
  • Sade Sati: running for Aquarius, Pisces and Aries Moons, with Saturn in Pisces.

December 2026 is a month of turning points. Rahu moves into sidereal Capricorn and Ketu into Cancer on December 5, Saturn stations direct in Pisces on December 10, and Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo on December 12. Venus holds its own sign, Libra, all month. There is no eclipse.

All dates below are US Eastern time, calculated in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanamsa, mean node). For what this means for you personally, read it from your Moon sign first, then your rising sign. Not sure of either? Find your sidereal Moon sign.

What's the big picture in December 2026?

Three slow planets do the heavy lifting this month, and all three change gear within a week.

The nodes go first. Rahu and Ketu have spent the last stretch on the Aquarius and Leo axis, the axis of networks and crowds against personal pride and leadership. They move into Capricorn and Cancer, where the pull shifts to work, status and structure on one side and home, family and emotional safety on the other. We wrote a full guide to the Rahu Ketu transit from 2026 to 2028.

Then Saturn. Saturn has been retrograde in Pisces, in Uttara Bhadrapada, the nakshatra it rules. It turns direct and spends the rest of the month almost motionless. Saturn's sight in Vedic astrology falls on the 3rd, 7th and 10th signs from it, so in Pisces it watches Taurus, Virgo and Sagittarius. Sade Sati, Saturn's seven-and-a-half-year passage over the natal Moon, is running for Aquarius (last phase), Pisces (peak) and Aries (first phase) Moons.

Then Jupiter. Jupiter sits in Leo in Magha, the Ketu-ruled nakshatra of ancestors and inheritance, alongside Mars. Mars and Jupiter together in one sign form what's traditionally called Guru-Mangala yoga: drive with a sense of purpose behind it. Jupiter turns retrograde and slowly backs toward Cancer through January.

What does Mercury in Scorpio mean on December 2?

Mercury enters Scorpio on December 2 and stays there until the 21st. Scorpio is a Mars sign, and Mercury here wants the real story, not the press release. Conversations go deeper and get more private. It's a good stretch for research, audits, therapy-style talk and reading the fine print. The catch is suspicion: Mercury in Scorpio can turn a simple question into an investigation. Ask directly before you assume.

What does Rahu entering Capricorn mean on December 5?

Rahu enters Capricorn and Ketu enters Cancer on December 5, around midday Eastern. They stay on this axis until June 2028.

Rahu in Capricorn, Saturn's sign, turns collective hunger toward institutions, government, careers and who holds power. It favours slow ambition: a plan, a skill, a structure built month by month. Ketu in Cancer loosens the grip on home, family and comfort, and asks what security actually means once the familiar props shift. Both start at the very end of their signs, Rahu in Dhanishta and Ketu in Ashlesha.

Node changes are rarely loud on the day. Notice what starts to feel urgent over the next few weeks in the house Capricorn rules in your chart, and what starts to feel less important in the house Cancer rules.

What does the New Moon in Scorpio mean on December 8?

The New Moon falls at 22°42' Scorpio in Jyeshtha on December 8, in the evening Eastern. Scorpio is the sign where the Moon is traditionally weakest, and Jyeshtha, ruled by Mercury, is the nakshatra of the eldest: seniority, responsibility, and protecting what's yours. Mars in Leo casts its 4th-house aspect onto Scorpio, which adds heat.

Collectively, this is a New Moon for honest reckonings, especially about power and who carries the load. Set intentions around boundaries and responsibility rather than big launches.

What does Saturn turning direct mean on December 10?

Saturn stations direct on December 10 at 13°41' Pisces, in Uttara Bhadrapada. The station is the slowest part of the cycle, so expect the days either side to feel heavy or stuck, especially in the house Pisces rules for you.

Once Saturn moves forward, delayed plans start moving again, at Saturn's pace. If you're in Sade Sati, this is the moment to pick back up whatever you set down during the retrograde, with fewer obligations and better boundaries.

What does Jupiter retrograde in Leo mean on December 12?

Jupiter stations retrograde on December 12 at 2°47' Leo, in Magha. Neptune turns direct at 7°22' Pisces the same evening, a quieter shift that tends to clear some fog.

Jupiter retrograde isn't a loss of luck. It's a review. Questions about teachers, beliefs, children, wealth and meaning go inward for a while. Because Jupiter backs into Cancer, its sign of exaltation, on January 24, 2027, think of this as the start of a return to unfinished business from earlier in 2026.

What does the Sun in Sagittarius mean on December 15?

The Sun enters Sagittarius on December 15, late in the evening Eastern, starting in Mula, the Ketu-ruled nakshatra of roots. Sagittarius is Jupiter's sign, and the Sun here receives Jupiter's 5th aspect from Leo and Saturn's 10th aspect from Pisces: confidence, with a demand for discipline.

This ingress, Dhanu Sankranti, begins what many Hindu calendars call Kharmas or Dhanur Masa, a month traditionally kept for devotion and study rather than weddings or major new ventures. Whatever your practice, it's a natural time for learning, teaching and planning next year's direction.

What does Mercury in Sagittarius mean on December 21?

Mercury follows the Sun into Sagittarius on December 21 and picks up the same aspects from Jupiter and Saturn. Thinking turns from detail to the big picture. Good for writing, publishing, travel plans and conversations about what you believe. Watch for overpromising; Mercury in Sagittarius can say yes before checking the calendar.

What does the Full Moon in Gemini mean on December 23?

The Full Moon peaks at 7°59' Gemini in Ardra on December 23, in the evening Eastern. Ardra is ruled by Rahu and linked to Rudra, the storm: tears that clear the air, a mess that has to be faced before it can be cleaned up. With the Sun opposite in Sagittarius, the tension is between the facts (Gemini) and the meaning you've made of them (Sagittarius).

It's a strong Full Moon for honest conversation, for finishing something you started writing, and for letting a feeling move through. If the holidays bring up grief or old family hurt, go gently, and lean on someone you trust or a counsellor if it gets heavy.

Love, money and the rest of December

Venus stays in Libra, its own sign, for the whole month, moving through Chitra, Swati and Vishakha before it reaches Scorpio on January 1. That's the most reliable thing in December's sky: relationships, beauty, negotiation and fairness all have a steady patron. Venus in Libra aspects Aries, which helps that sign's partnerships too.

Mars stays in Leo all month, moving forward but slowing. It is heading toward a retrograde station on January 10, 2027, so start any Mars-type project (fitness, a bold ask, a competitive push) early in December rather than late. From Leo, Mars aspects Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces, including Saturn, which can make the stretch around the Saturn station feel like pushing a heavy door.

Best days: the first days of the month, before the nodes shift; the stretch after the Sun reaches Sagittarius for study and planning. Slow days: December 10 to 12, while Saturn and Jupiter both sit at their stations.

Write this month

New Moon in Scorpio, December 8: What responsibility am I carrying that isn't mine? What would I protect if I stopped protecting everything?

Full Moon in Gemini, December 23: What did I learn this year that I haven't said out loud yet? Write it as plainly as you can, then write what it means to you.

For the node shift: Find the house Capricorn rules in your chart and the house Cancer rules. Write one thing you want to build in the first, and one thing you're ready to stop holding on to in the second.

Questions

Are there any eclipses in December 2026?

No. December 2026 has a New Moon and a Full Moon but no eclipse. The eclipse season that follows the nodes into Capricorn and Cancer begins in early February 2027, with a solar eclipse in Capricorn.

When does Saturn go direct in December 2026?

Saturn stations direct on December 10, 2026, at 13°41' Pisces in Uttara Bhadrapada, US Eastern time. Expect the few days around the station to feel slow; the forward motion shows up more clearly in the weeks after.

When does Jupiter go retrograde in 2026?

Jupiter stations retrograde on December 12, 2026, at 2°47' Leo in Magha. Retrograde Jupiter backs up into Cancer, its sign of exaltation, on January 24, 2027.

Is December 2026 a good month for marriage in Vedic astrology?

The first half is the easier window. Venus is strong in Libra all month, but once the Sun enters Sagittarius on December 15 many Hindu calendars begin Kharmas, a month traditionally avoided for weddings. For an actual muhurta, ask an astrologer who can read both charts.

Which Moon signs feel December 2026 the most?

Cancer and Capricorn Moons feel the node change most directly, since Ketu and Rahu move onto their 1st and 7th houses. Aquarius, Pisces and Aries Moons are in Sade Sati, so Saturn's station on December 10 lands harder for them.
