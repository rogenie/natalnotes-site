No. Each page gives you one affirmation and room to write. No homework, no fill-in-the-blanks. The sign pages on this site give you a prompt if you want one.

Natal Notes reads the sky sidereally, the Vedic way, where your Moon sign counts most, so that's a great place to start. But if your Sun sign is the one you've always recognized yourself in, write with that one. The finder above shows your Moon sign and both Sun signs.
