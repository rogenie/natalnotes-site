Questions

Is this a guided workbook?

No. Each page gives you one affirmation and room to write. No homework, no fill-in-the-blanks. The sign pages on this site give you a prompt if you want one.

Should I get my tropical sign or my sidereal sign?

Natal Notes reads the sky sidereally, so we'd start with your sidereal sign. But if your tropical sign is the one you've always recognized yourself in, that's the one to write with. Use the finder above to see both.

Where do I buy it?

Every journal is printed and shipped by Amazon. The buttons on this site go straight to its Amazon page.

Will affirmations change my life?

We won't promise that. They're a daily practice for changing how you talk to yourself. What you do with that is the part that's yours.
