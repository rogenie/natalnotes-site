Why there are two zodiacs

The tropical zodiac, used by most Western horoscopes, starts Aries at the spring equinox. It follows the seasons. The sidereal zodiac, used in Vedic astrology and by Natal Notes, lines the signs up with the constellations where they appear in the sky now.

Two thousand years ago the two lined up. Because the Earth's axis wobbles slowly (astronomers call it precession), they drift apart by about one degree every 72 years. Today the gap is roughly 24 degrees, which is why sidereal dates run about three weeks later.

Why does Vedic astrology use the Moon sign?

In Vedic astrology the Moon stands for the mind, so your Moon sign (your rashi) is how you feel and react day to day. It's what people in India usually mean by "my sign", monthly horoscopes there are written by Moon sign, and transits are traditionally counted from the natal Moon. Your rising sign is the foundation of the whole birth chart, and the Sun sign matters least. Our monthly horoscopes are written for your Moon sign first and your rising sign second.

Which zodiac is right?

Both are real systems with long histories, and astrologers disagree about which to use. We read the sky sidereally, and our monthly forecasts use the sidereal zodiac. Use the sign that helps you see yourself more clearly.
