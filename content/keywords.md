# Sidereal keyword map (Google autocomplete, US, researched 2026-11-22)

Autocomplete shows what people actually type; it doesn't give volumes. Treat the ordering
below as relative demand, not numbers.

## What people search
- **Sidereal basics (evergreen, highest repeat demand):** sidereal astrology chart / calculator / dates /
  birth chart / meaning / app / compatibility; sidereal zodiac dates / signs / calendar;
  what is my sidereal sign / vedic sign / sidereal rising / sidereal moon sign.
- **Sidereal vs tropical:** which is more accurate, reddit, dates, chart. People want a verdict and a fair answer.
- **Per sign:** "sidereal scorpio dates / rising / moon / traits / tropical sagittarius", "vedic scorpio dates /
  rising / ascendant / ruler", "sidereal leo rising / dates / season". Rising and moon are searched as much as sun.
- **Monthly horoscopes:** "vedic monthly horoscope", "monthly vedic horoscope 2026", "vedic astrology monthly
  predictions", "scorpio monthly vedic horoscope", "cancer monthly vedic horoscope", "moon sign horoscope monthly",
  "<sign> horoscope <month> <year>". "sidereal monthly horoscope" has no suggestions yet: low competition, so
  titles carry both words: "<Sign> Sidereal Horoscope <Month Year>: Vedic Monthly Forecast".
- **Transits (big, date-driven):** saturn transit 2027 (vedic, dates, nakshatra, 2027 to 2029),
  jupiter transit 2027 (vedic, dates, retrograde, in which rashi, in leo), rahu ketu transit 2027 (to 2028/2029),
  vedic astrology 2027 / predictions 2027 / saturn in aries 2027, eclipse 2027.
- **Monthly sky:** "january 2027 astrology transits", "full moon january 2027 astrology", "vedic astrology forecast".
- **Authority benchmark:** Joni Patry (2026 predictions, birth chart calculator, galactic planner).
  Her format: exact transit dates, degrees and nakshatras, then house-by-house readings by rising sign.
  We match the depth, never the wording.
